## 🚀 About Me

I turn messy, multi-table data into clear numbers people can act on. I'm finishing an **MSc in Data Science** at the University of Hertfordshire, on top of a **BSc (Hons) in Physics**, and before that I spent several years in operations roles where tracking KPIs and fixing bottlenecks was the day job.

- 🔍 **What I do best:** cleaning and joining relational data, exploratory analysis, customer segmentation, and reporting that non-technical stakeholders can read at a glance.
- 🧰 **Day to day:** SQL, Excel (XLOOKUP, pivots, SUMIFS) and Python (Pandas, NumPy) in Jupyter.
- 🏗️ **On the side:** I build web apps and Discord bots for fun, which keeps my Git and JavaScript habits sharp.
- 💼 **Looking for:** Junior MI Analyst, Reporting Analyst and Operations Data roles. Available full-time from **1 November 2026** (UK Graduate Route visa).
